# Known issues

- PS-return is experimental in DEV; repeated/game-transition reliability is
  unresolved. TESTER excludes it. Original dashboard flashes may remain.
- HEN auto-start is not a verified working feature.
- External launches, metadata/art and storage readings need testing per setup.
- Linux installer, party voice chat, NEXT report integration and native local
  PKG installation are not delivered as complete features.
- DEV and TESTER share NOVA00001; installing one replaces the other.
- Prior DEV ShellUI errors remain unresolved. Stop the experiment on an error
  and preserve diagnostics; do not repeatedly force it past failures.
- Optional music/theme assets have an outstanding redistribution review.
  NovaCore does not claim ownership or a blanket licence over upstream assets.
